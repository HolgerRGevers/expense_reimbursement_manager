## §10 Workflow State Machine

### Status Enumeration

- **Draft** — pre-submission UI state; no workflow transitions lead back to Draft.
- **Submitted** — claim has passed on_validate; initial routing pending.
- **Pending LM Approval** — awaiting Line Manager tier-1 decision; SLA 3 days.
- **Pending HoD Approval** — escalated to Head of Department; either over tier-1 threshold or self-approval bypass.
- **Pending Second Key** — HoD (Key 1) approved; awaiting Finance Director (Key 2) dual-approval.
- **Key 2 Dispute** — Finance Director rejected; claim returned to HoD for reconsideration.
- **Approved** *(terminal)* — fully approved; no further transitions unless resubmission edit.
- **Rejected** *(terminal)* — rejected at LM or HoD tier; employee may edit and resubmit.
- **Resubmitted** *(re-entrant)* — employee has edited a rejected claim; re-enters routing cycle.

### Transitions

| from_status | trigger | condition | to_status | actor_role | workflow_ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Draft" | form_submit | POPIA consent = true; all validations pass | "Submitted" | Employee | → WF-02 |
| "Submitted" | form_submit | submitter holds Line Manager role | "Pending HoD Approval" | Employee | → WF-03.B |
| "Submitted" | form_submit | submitter does not hold Line Manager role | "Pending LM Approval" | Employee | → WF-03.A |
| "Pending LM Approval" | lm_approve | amount_zar ≤ tier-1 threshold | "Approved" | Line Manager | → WF-07.A |

### WF-02  expense_claim.on_validate

- Trigger: expense_claims.on_validate (Employee | form submission before save)
- Guard:   always
- Effect:  Enforces eight hard-stop validation gates: (1) Expense_Date not in future; (2) Expense_Date not older than 90 days; (3) amount_zar > 0; (4) Supporting_Documents not null; (5) duplicate detection by date+amount+submitter; (6) claims >= R5,000 require Full Tax Invoice VAT type; (7) POPIA_Consent must be true. - Writes:  input.status := "Submitted"; input.Submission_Date := zoho.currenttime; input.Retention_Expiry_Date := zoho.currenttime + 5 years
- APIs:    —
- Emails:  —

## §14 Business Rules Index

**BR-001** (governance) No user in the Line Manager role may approve their own submitted claim — enforced_by: WF-03.B, WF-06

**BR-002** (governance) Line Manager submitters are automatically routed to HoD tier to prevent self-approval (King IV P1) — enforced_by: WF-03.B, WF-06

**BR-003** (governance) All expense claim state transitions are recorded in approval_history with actor, timestamp, and context to ensure complete audit traceability — enforced_by: WF-03.A, WF-03.B, WF-06, WF-07.A, WF-07.B, WF-08, WF-09.A, WF-09.B, WF-10, WF-11.A, WF-11.B, WF-12, WF-13

**BR-005** (governance) The Key 2 approver (Finance Director) must not be the same person as Key 1 approver (HoD); same-person attempt is blocked and logged (King IV P7) — enforced_by: WF-11.A

**BR-006** (compliance) At least one supporting document (receipt or invoice) must be attached to satisfy SARS S11(a) substantiation requirements — enforced_by: WF-02 (form_validation)

**BR-007** (compliance) Claims with amount_zar >= R5,000 require VAT_Invoice_Type = "Full Tax Invoice (>= R5,000)" per the SARS VAT Act — enforced_by: WF-02 (form_validation)

**BR-008** (compliance) POPIA_Consent must be true before claim submission is accepted — enforced_by: WF-02 (form_validation)

**BR-009** (compliance) Retention_Expiry_Date must be set to Submission_Date + 5 years per SARS Tax Administration Act S29 — enforced_by: WF-02

**BR-010** (compliance) Expense_Date must not be older than 90 days from current date at submission — enforced_by: WF-02 (form_validation)

**BR-011** (financial) amount_zar must be greater than zero — enforced_by: WF-02 (form_validation)

**BR-012** (financial) Expense_Date must not be in the future — enforced_by: WF-02 (form_validation)

**BR-013** (financial) Claims within the tier-1 threshold (default R999.99) receive final approval from the Line Manager; claims above are escalated to HoD — enforced_by: WF-07.A, WF-07.B, config

**BR-014** (financial) Claims exceeding the dual-approval threshold (default R5,000.00) require both Key 1 (HoD) and Key 2 (Finance Director) authorization — enforced_by: WF-09.B, config

**BR-015** (financial) GL code is auto-assigned based on expense category lookup during LM or HoD approval; employees do not manually set GL code — enforced_by: WF-07.A, WF-07.B, WF-09.B, role_permission

**BR-016** (sla) Claims awaiting LM approval for 2 days receive a reminder email to the LM (CC HoD) — enforced_by: WF-13

**BR-017** (sla) Claims awaiting LM approval for 3 days are automatically escalated to HoD status (SLA auto-escalation) — enforced_by: WF-13

**BR-018** (sla) Claims awaiting Key 2 (Finance Director) approval for 3 days escalate to admin/CEO for manual intervention — enforced_by: WF-13

**BR-019** (data_integrity) Duplicate claims (same employee, expense date, and amount) trigger a warning at submission but do not block the claim — enforced_by: WF-02

**BR-020** (data_integrity) Parent_Claim_ID is a self-referential pick list on expense_claims used to link resubmitted claims to their parent record; the self-ref edge must be configured as pick list filtered to expense_claims.ID — enforced_by: WF-06, form_validation

**BR-021** (data_integrity) Version number increments on every resubmission; dual-approval state is fully reset so the new version cycles through fresh approval routing — enforced_by: WF-06
